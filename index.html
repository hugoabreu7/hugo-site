Delivering Across Borders

A Practical Guide to Building and Running Delivery Teams in Several Countries

Cross-border delivery usually fails when headquarters and the local team stop understanding each other, and nobody notices until a client does.

This book condenses fifteen years of field notes into a practical manual for running distributed teams. It stays close to real accounts, hard trade-offs, and mistakes I paid for: choosing a country with data, protecting margins across currencies, handling scope growth, recovering troubled accounts, and governing AI-assisted engineering teams.

  • 10 Practical Chapters
  • Chapter Checklists
  • 4 Ready-to-Use Templates
  • Zero Consulting Jargon
Pillar 01 · Culture

Spot misalignment early

Read quiet signals across time zones, catch blame before it turns into turnover, and adapt to local working rhythms without losing standards.

Pillar 02 · Knowledge

Kill information silos

Structure short daily overlap windows, Architecture Decision Records, and clear handovers so remote teams stop guessing while headquarters sleeps.

Pillar 03 · Process

Make governance hold under pressure

Close the gap between written rules and panic responses with clear ownership, written business sign-offs, blameless post-mortems, and steady crisis routines.

Who this book is for

Written as a direct, practical manual you can use on Monday morning, whether you already own the P&L or are heading toward that seat.

Delivery Managers & Engineering Leads

Concrete habits for managing scope trade-offs, estimating honestly, running remote technical interviews, and keeping stakeholders aligned across time zones.

Founders & Managers Expanding Abroad

How to evaluate countries objectively, use turnkey partners to land fast, set clear boundaries between HQ and local teams, and protect margins.

Account, Program & Recruiting Peers

The operational view from the delivery desk: how contracts shape daily risk, how to define seniority before searching, and how to save a troubled account.

Management Students

An honest look at international delivery beyond textbooks, including practical advice on how to position yourself for your first assignment abroad.
